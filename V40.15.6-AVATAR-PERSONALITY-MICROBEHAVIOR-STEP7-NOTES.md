# Math12 Hub V40.15.6 — Avatar Personality & Micro-behavior (Step 7)

Built directly from V40.15.5. Steps 1–6 are preserved.

## Step 7 changes
- Context-aware behavior profiles: idle, studio, study, focus, thinking, success, recovery, room, window, relax.
- Eyes now lead subtle head motion instead of moving independently.
- Attention targets: user, content, book, board, left/right, window and trophy.
- Long-interval micro behaviors: tiny glance, micro nod, posture reset and thinking glance. They are deliberately restrained and delayed after user activity.
- Exam/practice context emits non-scoring learning events: session started, question viewed, answer input and session ended. No correctness is exposed before submission.
- Emotion Engine and Motion Engine cooperate: focus/thinking/success/recovery facial states automatically select matching movement behavior.
- Math Room presence maps study/window/relax positions to matching gaze and body behavior.
- Reduced-motion and low-power modes continue to suppress decorative motion.
- New public APIs: AvatarMotion.setBehavior(), restoreBehavior(), lookAt(), clearAttention(), markActivity().

## Compatibility
No avatar identity, wardrobe, item IDs, face schema, room data schema or Firestore structure changed.
